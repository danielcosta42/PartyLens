# PartyLens: a group radar for WoW: Forever

PartyLens listens to the LookingForGroup channel and to other PartyLens users, and shows every group that is forming in one panel: what it runs, which roles it still needs, how fresh it is and who leads it. Whisper or invite with one click.

## Features

- **The radar.** Every group lights up as a blip; new ones pop in as they appear.
- **What each group still needs.** Tank, healer and dps pips, how full the group is, and the activity, including the Forever dungeons (City of Dalaran, Ruins of Lordaeron, Excavation Site: Wetlands, The Hall of Thanes, Manor Mistmantle).
- **Filters.** Your roles, LFM or LFG, activity, text search, open groups only, spam hidden.
- **One-click actions.** Whisper with a message built from your roles, Invite, Who, Block.
- **Announce.** Build your post, send it to LookingForGroup and publish it to the PartyLens network with one click.
- **Autopilot suggestions.** Players from the radar who fill the roles you are missing. You confirm every invite and whisper.
- **Layers.** Every layer heard, zone by zone, with its population, beacons on duty and news. Go to one and a beacon there invites you.
- **Be a beacon.** On by default: bring players to your layer with one click per invite and climb from Spark to Legend.
- **Summon.** See who in your group needs a summon and announce it.

## Commands

- `/pl` or `/partylens`: open or close the window
- `/pl announce`, `/pl layer`, `/pl help`

## How it works

PartyLens shares ChehulNet with GuildOS and Lodestar: guild, group and a hidden member list that lets users reach a few others of the faction. The game requires a real click for invites, visible chat and /who, so every one of those comes from a button you press. No dependencies.

## Em português

O PartyLens escuta o canal LookingForGroup e os outros usuários do PartyLens e mostra num painel todo grupo que está se formando: o que vai fazer, que funções faltam, há quanto tempo e quem lidera. Whisper ou convite em um clique.

- **Radar** com um ponto para cada grupo; os novos acendem quando chegam.
- **Pips de tanque, cura e dano**, vagas e atividade, incluindo as masmorras novas do Forever.
- **Filtros** por função, LFM/LFG, atividade, busca, só abertos e sem spam.
- **Anunciar**: monta o post, manda no LookingForGroup e publica na rede com um clique.
- **Autopilot (sugestão)**: jogadores do radar que fecham as funções que faltam; você confirma cada convite.
- **Camadas**: cada camada ouvida, por zona, com população, beacons de plantão e novidades. Escolha uma e um beacon de lá te convida.
- **Seja um beacon**: ligado por padrão; traga jogadores para a sua camada com um clique por convite e suba de Faísca a Lenda.
- **Invocação**: quem do grupo precisa de summon, anunciado com um clique.

Comandos: `/pl`, `/pl announce`, `/pl layer`, `/pl help`.
